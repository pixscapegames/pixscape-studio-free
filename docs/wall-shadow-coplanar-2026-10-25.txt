WALL SHADOWS: COPLANAR EXCLUSION GROUPS — 2026-10-25

Scope
Production geometry, alpha, normals, biases, receivers and shadow attachments
are unchanged. Groups now join only continuations of the same straight physical
strip. Same-owner continuations still require the same structure/base/height and
contact; cross-owner storeys still require matching metrics, touching altitudes
and positive longitudinal overlap. Perpendicular returns remain independent.
Rounded occurrences retain their own exclusion and no longer bridge both walls.
No block number is used in the production rule. Preparation/invalidation remains
at the existing static geometry publication boundary; no new per-frame work.

Limits
No full-scene orbit, no exhaustive full test suite, no performance benchmark.

support an exact reconstruction of each sprite. Stepped alpha boundaries can
remain visible. Unusual thickness changes, square supports, and other rounded
poses need broader visual coverage before claiming universal equivalence.
